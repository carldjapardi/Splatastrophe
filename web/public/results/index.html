Sharper detail at full resolution

From the same held-out angle, the longer, full-resolution run shows cleaner column edges and more defined stonework. Early haze around the roof and openings recedes. The finest lettering is still soft.

A little pavement under the columns

Adding a narrow strip retained more of the contact area beneath the gate. The base is still patchy, and a bright stray patch remains near the center.

Ghosting at an oblique angle

We removed splats outside a 3D volume around the gate. The large detached clouds beside and above it disappear from this angle. Some haze remains close to the columns and base.

The 96-photo pilot has 82 cameras in a front-facing 45° sector and 14 in the adjacent sector. The full 774-photo split also covers only the front and two front-oblique sectors. This helps explain why the shape becomes less solid as the camera rotates away from the front.

Issues and fixes tried

  • Sky and plaza clutter — foreground masks and Spirula’s cutout loss cleared most of the background.
  • Soft stone detail — full-resolution training sharpened the relief and columns, though the finest markings remain soft.
  • Detached ghosting — a 3D crop removed large floating clouds and kept 95.8% of the splats.
  • Floating column bases — a narrow pavement mask filled in some of the base, which remains patchy.
  • Thin side views — extending selected front splats in depth preserved the stone color but made little visible difference.
  • Browser blur — using the viewport’s actual pixel resolution restored fine detail.
